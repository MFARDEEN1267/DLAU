Avengers:Doomsday Rumors and Updates

  • According to recent leaks, Doctor Doom will reportedly be flanked by three hooded "Latverian Witches" and a mysterious ally named "Boris".
    Fans heavily speculate that Boris is actually a disguised Doctor Strange, as his mask features an eye-like slot that matches the Sorcerer Supreme's newly acquired third eye.
  • Following playful interruptions and lighthearted comedic skits from Ryan Reynolds and Hugh Jackman across major events like San Diego Comic-Con and D23, heavy industry speculation suggests Deadpool and Wolverine are virtually guaranteed to appear in Avengers: Doomsday. Multiple reports point to a extraordinarily high probability of the duo making at least a cameo or opening appearance to help bridge Marvel's expanding multiverse storyline.
